# SampleTank Sound Module

**SampleTank Sound Module** is a powerful software sampler and sound workstation for music producers and composers. As a comprehensive **SampleTank Virtual Instrument**, it runs as a VST, AU, or AAX plugin inside any major DAW. This **SampleTank Music Production** platform comes with over 8,000 instruments including acoustic pianos, orchestral strings, vintage synths, and electronic drum kits. With **SampleTank Sampler Workstation**, you can import your own WAV, AIFF, and MP3 samples, then map, loop, and stretch them across the keyboard.
